The node-level entry is the formal counterpart of
Corollary~\ref{cor:v8-preferences-pass-to-root}. Let \(u\) be any realized
subtree with raw span \(S(u)\), and let \(z\) be a realized state in the support
of the reduction at \(u\). Under exact local laws, \(z\) and \(S(u)\) are in the
same oracle fiber. Therefore every oracle-compatible readout has the same value
on the state and on the raw span.
