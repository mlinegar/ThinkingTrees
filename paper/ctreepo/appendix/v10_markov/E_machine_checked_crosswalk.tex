The node-level entry for
Corollary~\ref{cor:v8-preferences-pass-to-root} is the formal
counterpart in Lean: for any realized subtree with raw span
\(S(u)\), every realized state \(z\) at \(u\) sits in the same
oracle fiber as \(S(u)\) under exact local laws, so every
oracle-compatible readout takes the same value on the state and on
the raw span.
