
\section{LDA Replication as a Unified-\texorpdfstring{$g$}{g} Tree}
\label{app:lda-replication}

This appendix records the exact deterministic part of the LDA replication
claim. The input object for ordinary LDA is a bag of words, not raw semantic
text. Text only enters through a tokenizer and vocabulary map that produce a
histogram. Once a document is represented as leaf histograms, the tree
operation is the usual sufficient-statistic aggregation. In likelihood terms,
the same construction says that leaf likelihoods multiply, leaf
log-likelihoods add, and average log-likelihoods combine by token-weighted
averaging.

\subsection{Bag Leaves and Additive Statistics}

Let a document be split into leaves $\ell=1,\ldots,L$. For vocabulary $V$,
write the leaf bag as
\[
  c_\ell \in \mathbb{N}^{V},
  \qquad
  N_\ell = \sum_{v\in V} c_\ell(v).
\]
The full-document bag is
\[
  c_d = \sum_{\ell=1}^L c_\ell,
  \qquad
  N = \sum_{v\in V} c_d(v) = \sum_{\ell=1}^L N_\ell.
\]
Thus the empirical document word distribution is exactly the token-weighted
average of the leaf word distributions:
\begin{align}
  \hat p_d(v)
  &= \frac{c_d(v)}{N}
   = \frac{\sum_\ell c_\ell(v)}{N} \\
  &= \sum_\ell \frac{N_\ell}{N}\frac{c_\ell(v)}{N_\ell}.
\end{align}

For topic statistics, suppose each token or word occurrence has a hard or soft
topic responsibility $q_t(k)$, with $k\in\{1,\ldots,K\}$. For a leaf, define
\[
  T_\ell(k) = \sum_{t\in \ell} q_t(k),
  \qquad
  R_\ell(v,k) = \sum_{t\in \ell} \mathbf{1}\{w_t=v\}q_t(k).
\]
The document totals are additive:
\[
  T_d(k)=\sum_\ell T_\ell(k),
  \qquad
  R_d(v,k)=\sum_\ell R_\ell(v,k).
\]
Consequently the normalized document topic proportion is exactly
\begin{align}
  \hat\pi_d(k)
  &= \frac{T_d(k)}{N}
   = \sum_\ell \frac{N_\ell}{N}\frac{T_\ell(k)}{N_\ell},
\end{align}
and the normalized word-topic joint statistic is exactly
\begin{align}
  \hat J_d(v,k)
  &= \frac{R_d(v,k)}{N}
   = \sum_\ell \frac{N_\ell}{N}\frac{R_\ell(v,k)}{N_\ell}.
\end{align}
The topic-conditional word distribution uses topic-mass weights rather than
token-mass weights:
\begin{align}
  \hat\phi_d(v\mid k)
  &= \frac{R_d(v,k)}{T_d(k)}
   = \sum_\ell \frac{T_\ell(k)}{T_d(k)}
      \frac{R_\ell(v,k)}{T_\ell(k)}.
\end{align}
These equations are the algebraic meaning of ``document-level LDA statistics
are weighted averages of leaves.'' The numerator statistics add; the normalized
readout turns the sum into a weighted average.

\subsection{Likelihood and Average Log-Likelihood}

Fix a document topic mixture $\pi$ and topic-word matrix $\phi$. The ordinary
bag-of-words LDA marginal word probability is
\[
  m_{\pi,\phi}(v) = \sum_k \pi(k)\phi_k(v).
\]
Ignoring the multinomial coefficient, the bag likelihood is
\[
  p(c_d\mid \pi,\phi)
  = \prod_{v\in V} m_{\pi,\phi}(v)^{c_d(v)}.
\]
Because $c_d(v)=\sum_\ell c_\ell(v)$,
\[
  p(c_d\mid \pi,\phi)
  = \prod_{\ell=1}^L p(c_\ell\mid \pi,\phi).
\]
Equivalently, with
\[
  \ell_{\pi,\phi}(v) = \log m_{\pi,\phi}(v),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{LL}_{\pi,\phi}(c)
    = \sum_{v\in V} c(v)\ell_{\pi,\phi}(v),
\]
the log-likelihood is an additive sufficient statistic:
\[
  \mathrm{LL}_{\pi,\phi}(c_d)
  = \sum_{\ell=1}^L \mathrm{LL}_{\pi,\phi}(c_\ell).
\]
Normalizing by token count turns this leaf sum into an exact weighted average.
Let $N_\ell=\sum_v c_\ell(v)$, $N=\sum_\ell N_\ell$, and
\[
  \overline{\mathrm{LL}}_{\pi,\phi}(c)
  = \frac{\mathrm{LL}_{\pi,\phi}(c)}{\sum_v c(v)}.
\]
Then
\[
  \overline{\mathrm{LL}}_{\pi,\phi}(c_d)
  = \sum_\ell \frac{N_\ell}{N}
    \overline{\mathrm{LL}}_{\pi,\phi}(c_\ell).
\]
Thus likelihood is multiplicative over leaf bags, log-likelihood is additive,
and average log-likelihood is token-weighted across leaves. This is the
likelihood-side version of the same aggregate-state decomposition.

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$f$}{f} and Unified \texorpdfstring{$g$}{g} Realization}

The tree-facing state space $X$ contains leaf bag/stat observations and
intermediate aggregate states. There is one shared endomorphism
\[
  g : X \to X,
\]
not one function for leaves and another for merges. Leaves and merge pairs are
embedded into $X$ first:
\[
  \iota_{\mathrm{leaf}} : S \to X,
  \qquad
  \iota_{\mathrm{merge}} : X\times X \to X.
\]
Then the same $g$ is applied at every node. In the exact LDA carrier,
\[
  g(\mathrm{bag}(s)) = \mathrm{state}(s),
  \qquad
  g(\mathrm{state}(s)) = \mathrm{state}(s),
\]
and
\[
  \iota_{\mathrm{merge}}(x,y)
  = \mathrm{state}\big(\mathrm{Merge}(\mathrm{stats}(x),\mathrm{stats}(y))\big).
\]
The induced fold satisfies
\[
  \mathrm{stats}(\mathrm{Fold}_g(T))
  = \sum_{\ell=1}^L s_\ell,
\]
where the right-hand side means the additive LDA sufficient statistics, plus
the explicit cross terms required for the outer-product co-occurrence sketch.
A document oracle/readout is then
\[
  f : X \to Y.
\]
For any document-level LDA target $f^\ast$ that factors through the aggregate
state, choose
\[
  f(x) = r(\mathrm{stats}(x)),
  \qquad
  f^\ast(s)=r(s).
\]
Then
\[
  f(\mathrm{Fold}_g(T)) = f^\ast(\mathrm{BagFold}(T)).
\]
This is the exact realizability claim: doc-level supervision against $f^\ast$
can be matched by some $f$ and the unified $g$ when the hypothesis class
contains this aggregate-state construction.

\subsection{Scope of the Claim}

This appendix proves the deterministic decomposition of the LDA sufficient
statistics and their normalized readouts. It does not say that independently
running a nonlinear collapsed LDA posterior on each leaf and averaging those
posteriors equals the collapsed posterior for the whole document. Collapsed
Bayesian posterior inference is nonlinear. The correct statement is that the
posterior, likelihood, topic proportions, and co-occurrence readouts factor
through the same aggregate bag/stat state; once that state has been summed by
the unified $g$ tree, the document-level readout $f$ sees exactly the same
sufficient statistics as the flat document computation.
