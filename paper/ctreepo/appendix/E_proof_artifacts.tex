\subsection{Assumption Alignment Notes}

\paragraph{(1) Law naming map (paper vs Lean).}
In Lean, the local-law names are
\[
\text{C1} \leftrightarrow \text{L1},\qquad
\text{C2} \leftrightarrow \text{L3},\qquad
\text{C3} \leftrightarrow \text{L2},
\]
as defined in \texttt{lean3/FormalProofs/OPT/LocalLaws.lean}.

\paragraph{(2) C3/context packaging difference.}
The paper presents C3 as a two-link merge chain with context compatibility
(Assumption~\ref{ass:context}) used explicitly in proofs.
Lean's \texttt{L2} is packaged directly as internal-node zero distortion
\[
E[D(\texttt{reduce }g\ \texttt{node},\ S(\texttt{node}))]=0,
\]
so some context propagation steps are absorbed into the local hypothesis form.
This is a presentation/packaging difference, not a change in the certified target.

\paragraph{(3) Boundedness in formal multi-round proofs.}
The main axiom-free Lean theorem \texttt{multi\_round\_proper} carries explicit boundedness
inputs \texttt{M, hM, hbound} for summability. The paper's narrative statements are matched
either by supplying these bounds directly or via the typeclass wrapper
\texttt{multi\_round\_typeclass} on bounded metric spaces.

\paragraph{(4) Audited bound (Theorem~\ref{thm:e2e}) vs Lean decomposition.}
The paper states the audited gap with explicit envelopes
$B_{\mathrm{cal}}, B_{\mathrm{est}}, B_{\mathrm{clip}}$ and event-level guarantees.
Lean's DSL layer formalizes both the deterministic envelope algebra and the
high-probability event composition, including
\texttt{treepo\_gap\_with\_calibration\_and\_estimation},
\texttt{treepo\_gap\_with\_calibration\_estimation\_clipping},
\texttt{dsl\_abs\_gap\_bound\_from\_estimate\_high\_prob}, and
\texttt{dsl\_abs\_gap\_bound\_from\_clipped\_estimate\_high\_prob}
in \texttt{lean3/FormalProofs/DSL/TreeIPW.lean}.
The paper-level $B_{\mathrm{cal}},B_{\mathrm{est}},B_{\mathrm{clip}}$ notation
is a naming layer over these formal objects.

\paragraph{(5) Fixed-partition theorem status in Lean.}
Appendix~\ref{app:fixed-partition} is represented in Lean as a deterministic-instantiation corollary of
\texttt{multi\_round\_proper}; the named re-export is
\texttt{fixed\_partition\_extension\_instantiation} in \texttt{OPT/MainTheorems.lean}.
This is a packaging choice; the assumption profile is the same (local laws on realized calls plus boundedness side conditions when required).

\paragraph{(6) Distortion-symbol mapping note.}
Lean distinguishes document-level $\Delta_R$ objects (see \texttt{coupling\_delta\_eq\_delta\_r\_zr}
and \texttt{delta\_r\_zr\_zero\_of\_local\_laws} re-exported in
\texttt{OPT/MainTheorems.lean}) from generic audited-population means used in DSL envelopes.
The paper follows the same distinction: audited unit-population means $\mu_{\mathrm{dist}}^\star,\mu_{\mathrm{dist}}^J$
coincide with $\Delta_R$ only under an explicit unit/coupling identification.
