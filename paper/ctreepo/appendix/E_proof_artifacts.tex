\subsection{Paper-to-Formalization Notes}

A few places in the paper use slightly different packaging than the
formalization does; these notes record the translation.

\paragraph{Law naming.} The paper's C1/C2/C3 correspond to the
formalization's L1/L3/L2 respectively:
\[
\text{C1} \leftrightarrow \text{L1},\qquad
\text{C2} \leftrightarrow \text{L3},\qquad
\text{C3} \leftrightarrow \text{L2}.
\]
The swap between C2/C3 and L2/L3 reflects an older ordering convention
inside the formalization and is purely cosmetic.

\paragraph{C3 vs L2 packaging.} The paper states C3 as a two-link merge
chain that uses context compatibility
(Assumption~\ref{ass:context}) explicitly in proofs. The formalization
packages L2 directly as internal-node zero distortion,
$E[D(\mathrm{reduce}\,g\,\mathrm{node},\,S(\mathrm{node}))]=0$, absorbing
some context-propagation steps into the local hypothesis form. This is
a presentation choice; the certified target is the same.

\paragraph{Distortion symbols.} The formalization distinguishes the
document-level $\Delta_R$ from the generic audited-population means
$\mu_{\mathrm{dist}}^\star,\mu_{\mathrm{dist}}^J$ that appear in the
envelope algebra of \S\ref{sec:estimation}. The two coincide only under
an explicit unit/coupling identification; the paper follows the same
distinction.

Build commands for the manuscript and the formalization live in
\texttt{paper/ctreepo/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}.
