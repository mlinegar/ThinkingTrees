\section{Proof-Assistant Crosswalk}
\label{app:v8-lean-crosswalk}

This appendix is the single Lean crosswalk. The mathematical proofs
are in Appendix~\ref{app:v8-full-proofs}; nothing in the body or the
other appendices depends on the Lean side. Reading this appendix is
optional and confers no theorem or estimate beyond the paper proof
stack.

Separately from the written proof stack, the load-bearing theorems
have been formalized in the Lean theorem prover. The repository is
\path|lean3/| in the source tree; the canonical entry point is
\path|FormalProofs/OPT/MainTheorems.lean| and the full
theorem-name index is \path|lean3/docs/PAPER_TO_LEAN_MAP.md|. The
formalization is experimental in the sense that it represents a
parallel record of the same arguments rather than a replacement
for them, and the build is current at the time of writing. We will
note that C-TreePO has not been applied to audit its own proofs; the
calibration term \(B_{\mathrm{cal}}\) for that self-audit is presumed
nonzero.

Table~\ref{tab:v10-claim-status} records the evidence status of each
load-bearing claim. Every body theorem, proposition, and corollary has
a row, paired with the paper location of its statement and proof and
with a one-line description of its current evidence type.
Table~\ref{tab:v8-lean-crosswalk} then lists the named Lean surfaces
behind each row for readers who want to inspect the formalization
directly.

The active Lean build uses one named axiom outside the standard
mathematical foundations: \path|ExpectedGroupLossLipschitz| in
\path|FormalProofs/Axioms.lean|, which supplies the Lipschitz premise
behind expected group-loss transport for the named preference
methods. The local-law, IPW, root-preservation,
preference-scope, and Markov-count surfaces listed above do not
depend on this axiom.

The point of this appendix is the parallel record. The proofs that
matter for the paper's claims are in
Appendices~\ref{app:v8-objective-ipw}
and~\ref{app:v8-full-proofs}; the formalization is the work
of someone who wanted to be sure twice and did not, as already
noted, run a sampled audit on the audit.
