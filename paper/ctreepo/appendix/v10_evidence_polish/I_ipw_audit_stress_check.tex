\section{IPW Audit Stress Check}
\label{app:v10-ipw-stress}

The IPW stress ladder checks the audit-estimator component of the
certificate. The simulation fixes finite populations of tree-like
units, assigns document-nonseparable preference and violation losses,
samples units with known propensities, and compares the
logged-propensity estimator with the finite-population target.

\subsection{Design}

The large hard run covers \(4\) cases, \(7\) document counts, \(64\)
population seeds, and \(15{,}000\) sampling trials per cell. The
document counts are \(60,120,240,480,960,1{,}920,3{,}840\), giving
\(1{,}792\) raw rows and \(28\) summary rows. The cases include a
balanced additive control, a top-\(k\) spike design with adversarial
propensities, a boundary-adversarial hybrid design, and a severe
back-loaded quorum design. Bernoulli and without-replacement designs
are both represented.

Each cell reports preference-loss coverage, violation-rate coverage,
IPW bias, interval width, effective sample size, and propensity
diagnostics. The estimand is the finite-population mean for the
selected loss. Small propensities change variance and effective
sample size while leaving the target unchanged.

\subsection{Largest-Scope Slice}

Table~\ref{tab:v10-ipw-largest-slice} reports the \(3{,}840\)-document
slice. Bias remains close to zero across the hard designs. Coverage
stays near or above the nominal \(90\%\) target used by the run, while
the adversarial designs reveal themselves through wider intervals and
smaller effective sample sizes.

The hardest cells are useful because they separate centering from
precision. The boundary-adversarial case has minimum joint
propensity \(0.00091\) and maximum joint weight above \(1{,}000\);
the IPW violation bias remains \(2.6\times 10^{-6}\), while the
effective sample size falls to about \(7{,}206\). The top-\(k\)
spike case has effective sample size about \(666\), and its violation
interval is correspondingly wide. This is the behavior required by
Equation~\eqref{eq:v8-finite-sample-certificate}: hard designs widen
\(B_{\mathrm{est}}\) through the variance term, while correct logged
propensities keep the target centered.
