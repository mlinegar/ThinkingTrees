\section{Audit and Label-Budget Details}\label{app:min-audit}

\subsection{Audit Units and Propensities}

The audit units are realized leaves, stored summaries, and merge nodes. A leaf
unit checks C1, a stored-summary unit checks C2, and a merge unit checks C3.
Each unit has an inclusion probability $\pi_i>0$ determined by the audit
design. Propensities must be logged so the estimator targets the realized tree
population rather than the sampled subset.

These tree units are not the same as the document-sampling units used for the
root-observed Manifesto/Benoit benchmark. There, every sampled document has a
full-document expert target and the tree root score can be evaluated directly.
The tree-unit design below is needed when the claim is local-law preservation
inside the realized compression tree.

\subsection{Horvitz--Thompson Estimation}

For sampled indicator $Z_i$ and local distortion $Y_i$, the population mean is
estimated by
\[
\hat{\mu}_{\mathrm{HT}}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_i \frac{Z_i}{\pi_i}Y_i.
\]
The design may oversample long spans, high-risk nodes, or boundary-heavy
merges. Correctness requires positivity and logged propensities; efficiency
depends on the sampling design.

\subsection{Calibration and Local Labels}

The accessible judge may be a cheaper LLM or domain model rather than the
trusted expert oracle. Calibration bounds the difference between judge-measured
distortion and oracle-measured distortion. In the Manifesto economic setting,
global labels query the whole manifesto/root score, while local labels query
sampled leaves and merges. Both label types tighten the same certificate only
when they measure the same economic oracle-preservation property.

\subsection{Reporting Template}

A complete deployment report should state the tree partition, local-law
sampling design, logged propensities, local distortion estimate, calibration
residual, estimation envelope, clipping adjustment, and final objective-unit
gap bound. The reporting object records the probability space, the four
gap terms, the certificate formula, the three event bounds, and the
failure-budget split. Reporting root scores against full-document expert
targets is a valid empirical root-level result. Reporting a C-TreePO local-law
certificate additionally requires the tree-unit audit design and logged
propensities above.
