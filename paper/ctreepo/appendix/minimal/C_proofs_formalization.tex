\section{Full Proofs and Formalization Map}\label{app:min-proofs}

\subsection{Inductive Preservation}

The proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:one-pass} is structural induction on the tree.
At a leaf, C1 gives $g(b)\sim b$. At an internal node, the induction
hypothesis gives oracle equivalence for the two child summaries. Context
compatibility lets those child summaries replace the raw child spans inside
the parent context. C3 then says the stored parent summary preserves the raw
parent span. Repeating to the root gives zero root distortion.

For multiple rounds, C2 applies to stored summaries in the range of $g$. Once a
root summary is oracle-equivalent to the document, applying $g$ again leaves
the oracle value unchanged. Iteration gives preservation for every $R\ge 1$.

\subsection{Schedule Invariance}

For a fixed partition, any admissible binary schedule reduces contiguous
spans. If each realized leaf and merge call satisfies the local laws, each
schedule's root is oracle-equivalent to the same raw document. The
equivalence is modulo the oracle: two schedules can produce different
intermediate strings or byte layouts and still share a root readout.

\subsection{Preference Equivalence and Gap Bound}

Preference equivalence uses the application assumption bundles. For DPO these
bundle oracle-measurable current/reference policies with an oracle-indexed pair
generator; for GRPO they bundle the corresponding policy, ranker or reward, and
group-generator premises. Once those premises instantiate the generic theorem
surface, exact preservation makes the full-document and summary objectives
pointwise equal on the admissible class, so the full and summary argmin sets
coincide in the residual-zero case. For a noisy or approximate preference
object, the same equivalence records a uniform residual $\epsilon$, and exact
summary minimizers become $2\epsilon$-optimal for the full objective.

For approximate preservation, method transport gives
\[
|E_\pi(x)-E_\pi(z)|
\le C_{\mathrm{meth}}\,\metric(\fstar(x),\fstar(z)).
\]
Taking expectations over $(X,Z^{(R)}(X))$ yields
$|G_{\mathrm{meth}}|\le C_{\mathrm{meth}}\Delta_R$. The audited certificate
decomposes the unknown $\Delta_R$ into the deployed estimate plus calibration,
estimation, and clipping envelopes, giving the high-probability event
\[
\Pr\!\left(|G_{\mathrm{oracle}}|\ge
C_{\mathrm{meth}}\hat{\Delta}_R+B_{\mathrm{cal}}+B_{\mathrm{est}}+
B_{\mathrm{clip}}\right)\le\delta_{\mathrm{total}}.
\]
The economic-state language in the main text is the paper-level
sufficient-state reading of the oracle-factorization assumptions used
here; it is not a proof that such a state exists for political text.

\subsection{C2 Independence}

C2 is independent of C1 and C3. A one-pass tree can preserve every fresh leaf
and merge call while a later call on an already stored summary maps it to a
different oracle class. This is why re-summarization is its own audit unit
rather than a consequence of leaf and merge consistency.
