\section{Related Work and Extended Scope Notes}\label{app:min-related}

\paragraph{Sufficient statistics and mergeable summaries.}
The economic state in the main text is task-relative, in the spirit of
sufficient statistics and Blackwell sufficiency
\citep{Fisher1922,Blackwell1953,Torgersen1991}. The geometric object is the fiber
$\{x':\fstar(x')=\fstar(x)\}$: a valid summarizer is required to
preserve fiber membership but is allowed to discard within-fiber
variation. C1--C3 are the empirically auditable form of that
requirement on the realized leaves and merges of a deployed tree, and
the Lean development (\texttt{OracleSufficientCompression.lean},
\texttt{InformationSufficiency.lean}) records the formal version. The
state need not preserve every textual property; it must preserve the
properties visible to the target oracle and downstream objective.
The closest algorithmic ancestor is the mergeable-summary literature
\citep{Agarwal2013MergeableTODS}, with database algebraic aggregates, MUD
aggregation, and monoid-based production systems as related systems lineages
\citep{GrayEtAl1997DataCube,FeldmanEtAl2008MUD,BoykinEtAl2014Summingbird}.
C-TreePO inherits the state-merge-readout discipline but replaces
hand-designed state with learned summaries and runtime audits.

\paragraph{Sketches, private channels, and learned variants.}
Streaming sketches such as Count-Min, HyperLogLog, and quantile summaries show
why the mergeable object is usually state rather than the scalar answer
\citep{FlajoletEtAl2007,CormodeMuthukrishnan2005,GreenwaldKhanna2001}. The
size-estimation lineage makes the same point for reachability and related
set-union queries \citep{Cohen1997SizeEstimation}. Sketch-Flip-Merge
\citep{HehirTingCormode2023SFM} is the immediate predecessor in the private
mergeable lineage. It asks the same sufficiency question under a local-DP
channel between leaf and merge, and shows that only matched
randomizer-summarizer pairs keep the merged state in a parametric family the
cardinality estimator can invert. Learned Bloom filters and learning-based
frequency estimation show another path: use learned structure to improve
classical data structures while retaining explicit guarantees or fallback
mechanisms \citep{Mitzenmacher2018LearnedBloom,HsuEtAl2019Frequency}.
C-TreePO replaces the closed-form state with a learned $(f,g)$ pair under the
local laws, and replaces the closed-form sufficiency proof with an audit on
the realized tree.

\paragraph{Information bottleneck.}
The $\lambda$-weighted optimizer view is closest to an information bottleneck:
learn a compressed representation that preserves what matters for a target
variable \citep{TishbyPereiraBialek1999}. C-TreePO adds a compositional
constraint absent from the classical bottleneck statement: the representation
must remain sufficient after the tree's leaf, merge, and re-summary calls.

\paragraph{Social-science measurement.}
LLM-based political measurement can recover expert signals
\citep{BenoitEtAl2025}. Design-based supervised learning warns that surrogate
labels can bias downstream estimates when errors interact with the estimand
\citep{EgamiEtAl2024}. C-TreePO addresses the compression side of the same
problem by auditing whether the representation being labeled still carries the
target judgment.

\paragraph{Long-context and retrieval methods.}
Longer context windows and RAG reduce truncation pressure but do not certify
what information was lost or distorted. C-TreePO can be combined with those
methods; its distinct claim is the local-law check plus design-based
certificate.

\paragraph{Tree-based text composition.}
Tree-structured compression and generation methods provide useful composition
machinery
\citep{KnightMarcu2000SentenceCompression,BarzilayMcKeown2005SentenceFusion,ClarkeLapata2008ILPSentenceCompression,KuznetsovaEtAl2014TreeTalk}.
C-TreePO adds a task oracle, local laws, and downstream objective transport.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The framework is conditional. It applies when local equivalence is stable under
context and downstream supervision factors through the preserved oracle. It
should fail loudly, through audit distortion, when the target is style, tone,
persuasion, irony, or another property not localized by the chosen tree state.
