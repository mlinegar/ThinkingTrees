\title{C-TreePO: Compression Trees for Oracle-Preserving Long-Document Learning}
\author{Mitchell Linegar}

\begin{abstract}
C-TreePO treats political-manifesto measurement as a representation,
objective, and audit problem on a realized compression tree. A manifesto score
is global, but the evidence is dispersed across paragraphs, sections, and
qualifications. A C-Tree stores task-relative policy-evidence states at leaves
and internal merges; C-TreePO trains and audits the state map so that the root
score preserves the expert policy oracle. The target oracle \(f^\ast\) defines
root and local-law losses, a learned proxy \(\widehat f\) supplies dense
local losses in large-language-model (LLM) settings, and sampled node-level
\(f^\ast\) calls correct proxy bias with logged propensities. The main
application is Manifesto Project measurement against the Benoit et al.
expert-survey benchmark: root-observed C-Tree scores match frontier LLM
baselines while leaving readable local units for design-based node audit.
\end{abstract}
