\section{Discussion}\label{sec:min-discussion}

The theorems in Section~\ref{sec:min-guarantees} preserve the oracle when
the local laws hold, and the experiments in
Section~\ref{sec:min-manifesto-results} exercise that preservation against
expert-survey means on a real political-text benchmark. The remaining
sections of the discussion state the finite-sample trade-off in plain
language, place the global-versus-local label split in context, and sketch
the distillation chain the same audit certifies once node-level results land.

\subsection{The Finite-Sample Gap Decomposition}\label{sec:min-tradeoff}

Under the local laws, the finite-sample gap between a preference objective
on tree summaries and the corresponding full-document objective decomposes
into three non-negative pieces:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:min-discussion-bound}
|G|
\;\le\;
\underbrace{\hat{\Delta}_R}_{\text{empirical IPW estimate}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{B_{\mathrm{cal}}}_{\text{fixed residual}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{\text{SE}(n_{\mathrm{global}} + n_{\mathrm{local}})}_{\text{shrinking envelope}}.
\end{equation}
This is the decomposition the gap-bound theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:e2e}) gives on any audit-produced certificate. The three
pieces behave differently and pay attention to different design knobs.

The empirical term $\hat{\Delta}_R$ is an IPW union bound over sampled
leaves, merge nodes, and idempotence rounds. Equivalently, it is the
audit's empirical estimate of how far the realized approximation error
fails to vanish on the C1/C2/C3 subspace. It is zero when every sampled
unit passes its check and grows linearly with the realized violation rate.
The neural-operator route (Appendix~\ref{app:min-neural}) gives the
upstream interpretation: a practitioner chooses an approximation tolerance
on the realized calls of the tree and converts that tolerance, through
explicit transfer moduli, into per-law audit budgets.

The fixed residual $B_{\mathrm{cal}}$ is the irreducible projection gap
between the accessible judge and the trusted oracle. It depends on the
calibration set and the confidence level, and only on those. Adding
downstream labels does not shrink it. If the judge is the oracle,
$B_{\mathrm{cal}} = 0$; otherwise it is the residual of the measurement
instrument, and the audit reports it explicitly rather than absorbing it.

The envelope $\text{SE}(n_{\mathrm{global}} + n_{\mathrm{local}})$ is the
part labels pay for. The idealized rate $C/\sqrt{n+1}$ is non-negative,
bounded by the structural constant $C$, and strictly decreasing in $n$
whenever $C > 0$; the separate-monotonicity statement extends this to two
label types so that holding one fixed, adding the other still tightens the
envelope. Concretely, $n_{\mathrm{global}}$ counts full-document labels and
$n_{\mathrm{local}}$ counts sampled audit nodes; both feed the same sum.

The function-space view also clarifies what local-law training does. The
per-law-weighted oracle objective has one term fitting the oracle and a
$\lambda$-weighted local-law term pushing the realized operator toward the
local-law-constrained subspace. The endpoints make the interpretation
literal: $\lambda = 0$ is pure oracle risk and $\lambda = 1$ is pure
projection penalty. Treating local-law weights as a projection and treating
the approximation error as structured by C1/C2/C3 are the same formal
assumption (Appendix~\ref{app:min-proofs}). The HLL anchor starts inside
the local-law subspace by construction, the Markov tree learns an ordered
boundary-sensitive member of it, and the manifesto pipeline uses audits to
estimate how close the deployed LLM operator lands to it.

Long context windows do not remove that issue; they change where the
engineering pressure appears. A million-token window may cut hard
truncation, but it does not certify that a single read made the same
rubric decision an expert would have made after tracking evidence across
the whole document. The local architecture gives a different claim: if
small leaves and pairwise merges preserve the oracle-relevant evidence,
then the root measurement is stable under divide-and-conquer and the audit
has localized places to look when it is not. The Manifesto results are
meant in exactly that sense.

\subsection{Global and Local Labels}\label{sec:min-global-local}

The global/local split is what the envelope responds to. Replacing
some fraction of root labels by a
proportionate amount of local labels at matched token mass leaves root
accuracy essentially unchanged across a wide budget range, because the
envelope responds to the \emph{total} label count and not to the ratio of
its two components (Appendix~\ref{app:min-markov} reports this trade
quantitatively on the controlled changepoint task). Leaf-size invariance
is what makes the same trade meaningful in long-document settings: when
changing the leaf size leaves the root estimate stable, each smaller
leaf becomes another less expensive site for sampling and supervision
rather than a change in the estimand. The Manifesto plateau in
Section~\ref{sec:min-manifesto-results} is the same observation under
rubric-driven semantics.

\subsection{The Distillation Chain}\label{sec:min-distillation}

The audit + IPW machinery generalizes one more time. Three label tiers can
be stacked, with the same audit certifying each transition:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Gold human labels.} A small random sample of expert-coded
documents (or expert-coded spans, where they exist) sets the calibration
oracle and bounds $B_{\mathrm{cal}}$.
\item \textbf{Strong-LLM proxy.} A frontier or large open-weight LLM is
prompted or trained to score documents under the same rubric. An audit
against the gold sample reports the strong-proxy distortion in oracle
units; if the audited residual meets the application's threshold, the
strong proxy stands in for the gold judge on much larger corpora.
\item \textbf{Small-LM distillation.} Because the strong proxy is cheap
enough to query at every realized node (leaf, merge, idempotence), it
produces \emph{node-level} labels rather than only document-level labels.
A smaller language model is then distilled against those node-level
targets, with the same C1/C2/C3 audit certifying the smaller model's
realized operator.
\end{itemize}

The step where the chain pays off is the third one. A strong proxy queried
at the document level costs the same as the document-level gold standard
and adds no node-level supervision; queried at every realized node it
produces the local labels the audit's estimation envelope responds to. In the Manifesto
setting the gold tier is already in place: the Manifesto Project's expert
\emph{quasi-sentence} codings cover $2{,}157$ platforms ($\sim\!2.27$M
coded spans) and give ground-truth oracle values at every leaf and at
every internal-node union span. The strong proxy is the Gemma-4-31B
pipeline of Section~\ref{sec:min-manifesto-results}; the distilled small
proxy is implementation-ready (Appendix~\ref{app:min-manifesto} sketches
the wiring). Distillation results are pending and will be added on
convergence; the framing here is that the same algebra and the same audit
that preserve the root score under one rubric also certify the next-tier
distillation under the same rubric.

\subsection{Scope}\label{sec:min-scope}

Equation~\eqref{eq:min-discussion-bound} is conditional. It assumes the
local laws hold on the realized tree, which the audit checks. Tasks whose
oracle does not decompose into a locally checkable sufficient statistic
(rhetorical-tone or discourse-level semantics in which replacing a span
with a locally equivalent one changes how neighboring text is interpreted)
will show high C1/C3 violation rates under the audit, and the bound will
honestly report that the tree's root prediction is no better than a flat
baseline on such tasks. The framework does not always apply; it applies
when the audit says it applies.

\subsection{Applications in Social-Scientific Measurement}\label{sec:min-applications-discussion}

Many social-scientific measurement problems share the Manifesto shape:
rubrics with locally codable categories, span-grounded expert judgments,
and aggregates that decompose across sections, chapters, or acts.
Comparative-politics party-position scoring
\citep{BenoitEtAl2025,ManifestoProject2025a}, policy-agenda coding,
clinical narrative review, and legal-corpus analysis all fit this
description. The practical value of the framework in these settings is
that Equation~\eqref{eq:min-discussion-bound} turns long-document
annotation into an audit-budget problem: a fixed calibration set sets
$B_{\mathrm{cal}}$ once, and any combination of global and local labels
the researcher can collect tightens the envelope along the monotone curve
$C/\sqrt{n+1}$.
