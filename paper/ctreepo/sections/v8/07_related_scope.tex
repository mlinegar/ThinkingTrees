\section{Scope and Related Work}\label{sec:v8-scope-related}

\subsection{Scope}

C-TreePO is scoped to rubric-driven long-document measurement where the target
judgment is global, evidence appears in local spans, and a finite population
of leaf, state, and merge checks can be sampled with known propensities. Party
manifestos are the motivating case. The same structure can arise in policy
platforms, legislative records, clinical narratives, and book-length reports,
provided the target can be expressed as a task-relative state that survives
local replacement.

The guarantee is task-relative. It applies to readouts that respect the chosen
oracle interface. A C-Tree trained to preserve economic policy evidence does
not automatically preserve rhetoric, coalition strategy, or writing style.
Those quantities require either a wider oracle or a separate measurement
channel. The current Manifesto evidence is also tiered: the root-observed
benchmark is complete at the document-score tier, while the node-level
local-law certificate requires sampled local oracle labels.

\subsection{Design-Based LLM Measurement}

\citet{EgamiEtAl2024} show why high-quality surrogate labels are insufficient
for social-science inference when prediction error is correlated with the
estimand. Their design-based supervised-learning framework uses logged
sampling over trusted labels to make downstream inference valid. C-TreePO
applies the same discipline one layer earlier, at the compression step. The
audit unit is a realized tree node rather than only a final document label,
and the estimand is local-law distortion in the state map.

The Manifesto setting adds a construct-validity question. Expert-survey means,
Manifesto quasi-sentence codings, and LLM teacher traces are related
measurement objects, but they are not interchangeable without a declared
target. C-TreePO keeps the target \(f^\ast\), the proxy \(\widehat f\), and
the sampled correction channel separate.

\subsection{Long-Context and Retrieval Methods}

Extended-context models, sliding-window scoring, recursive summarization, and
retrieval-augmented generation reduce practical input-length pressure. They do
not provide a local certificate that a policy distinction survived
compression. For nonseparable manifesto targets, the decisive evidence may be
an interaction between a spending commitment and a later qualification. A
retrieval or long-context score may be accurate on average while still giving
no sampled account of which interactions were preserved. C-TreePO is a
certification layer for that question.

\subsection{Tree Text Compression}

Tree-structured text compression and fusion have a long history in natural
language processing \citep{KnightMarcu2000SentenceCompression,BarzilayMcKeown2005SentenceFusion,ClarkeLapata2008ILPSentenceCompression}.
\citet{KuznetsovaEtAl2014TreeTalk} compose descriptions through structured
tree fragments. This lineage treats composition and compression as a joint
problem. C-TreePO adds the task oracle and local laws: a compressed node is a
state for a specified policy measurement target, and the audit estimates
whether the state remained valid on the deployed tree.

\subsection{Sufficiency and Mergeable Summaries}

The state/readout distinction connects to sufficient statistics
\citep{Fisher1922,Blackwell1953} and mergeable summaries
\citep{Agarwal2013MergeableTODS}. Classical sketches such as HyperLogLog
\citep{FlajoletEtAl2007}, Count-Min \citep{CormodeMuthukrishnan2005}, and
Greenwald-Khanna quantiles \citep{GreenwaldKhanna2001} choose the state by
hand and prove the merge algebra once. Sketch-Flip-Merge extends this
discipline to local differential privacy \citep{HehirTingCormode2023SFM}.

C-TreePO uses the same encode-merge-readout discipline for learned semantic
states. Classical sketches are exact state-machine instances of the local-law
idea; the manifesto state is learned, inspectable text or structured evidence,
and its validity is estimated rather than proved algebraically.

\subsection{Preference Learning}

Direct Preference Optimization learns from pairwise preferences by
reparameterizing the Bradley--Terry likelihood
\citep{BradleyTerry1952,RafailovEtAl2023DPO}. Group-relative preference
objectives use richer ranking or reward structures. C-TreePO's preference
claim is conditional: summary-based and document-based objectives align only
when the preference input dependence is oracle-compatible. For manifesto
applications, this means the preference must depend on preserved policy
content rather than on formatting, fluency, or rhetoric outside the chosen
measurement target.

\subsection{Neural Operators and LLM Programs}

Neural operators and Fourier neural operators provide representation families
for maps between structured function spaces
\citep{Kovachki23NeuralOperator,Li21FNO}. In C-TreePO they are capacity
routes for the same state map \(g\). Prompt programs are the LLM analogue:
they implement the leaf and merge calls, while the local laws and audit decide
whether those calls produced a valid task-relative state. The representation
family supplies candidates; the certificate comes from the oracle, local laws,
and sampling design.
