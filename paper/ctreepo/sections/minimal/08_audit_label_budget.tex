\section{Audit and Label Budget}\label{sec:min-audit}

The audit treats a realized compression tree as a finite population of local
checks. Leaves check C1, stored summaries check C2, and merge nodes check C3.
A sampling design selects units with logged positive propensities; each sampled
unit is scored by an accessible judge or oracle; and a Horvitz--Thompson style
estimator recovers population-level distortion from those local observations.
Figure~\ref{fig:min-audit-sample} shows six example node-sample sets on
the same tree: different panels cover different parts of the tree, so the
distortion estimate is a weighted sum over whichever nodes the design
happens to draw.
This is distinct from ordinary root-level corpus evaluation. In the
Manifesto/Benoit benchmark, the sampled document is the unit and the
full-document expert target is observed at the root for every benchmark item.
That supports the external root-score correlations in
Section~\ref{sec:min-manifesto-results}. The local-law audit instead samples
tree units inside realized documents to certify preservation and label-budget
substitution.

For the Manifesto economic example, this is the formal version of using
paragraphs or sections instead of whole manifestos. A local label can ask
whether a span summary preserved the economic-policy evidence, whether a merge
preserved the economic score of the union span, or whether re-summarization
shifted the economic readout. These labels are cheaper and more numerous than
full-document expert labels. They tighten the same certificate only if they
measure the same oracle-preservation property the theorem needs.

The reported certificate decomposes the remaining risk as
\[
|G|
\le
C_{\mathrm{meth}}\hat{\Delta}_R+B_{\mathrm{cal}}+B_{\mathrm{est}}+B_{\mathrm{clip}}.
\]
$\hat{\Delta}_R$ is the observed local distortion. $B_{\mathrm{cal}}$ covers
the gap between the accessible judge and the trusted expert oracle.
$B_{\mathrm{est}}$ is sampling error. $B_{\mathrm{clip}}$ records any
stabilization bias from clipped inverse-propensity weights.
$C_{\mathrm{meth}}$ is the method transport constant that converts oracle
distortion into objective units. The reporting object bundles this
formula with the calibration, estimation, clipping, and failure-budget
events. The certificate is therefore honest about
surrogate-label risk: more local labels shrink the design-based uncertainty,
but they do not remove calibration error between the judge and the
social-science target. HLL is the clean limiting example of the same
accounting: a tree can preserve the register state exactly and still inherit
the estimator's known readout noise. In Manifesto measurement, the analogous
uncertainty is not a closed-form \(1.04/\sqrt{m}\) term but the calibration
gap between accessible local judgments and the expert economic oracle.
